# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and make space for
people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's
private information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents BedrockNexus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement
decisions should consider context, impact, previous behavior, and the safety of
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to a BedrockNexus organization owner or
repository maintainer. Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public
issue. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably
possible and will not retaliate against good-faith reports.
